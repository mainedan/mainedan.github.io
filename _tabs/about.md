Welcome to **Mainedan's Docs**—my personal notebook for documenting everything I build, break, and fix in my homelab.

## Who I Am

I'm Dan (Mainedan), a tech enthusiast from Maine with a soft spot for Linux, self-hosting, and figuring out how things work under the hood. What started as a way to keep track of my own configurations and troubleshooting notes has grown into a structured library of runbooks and guides.

## What You'll Find Here

This site is primarily a reference for my future self, but I keep it public in case it saves someone else a few hours of digging through logs. Most posts focus on:

* **Virtualization & Backups:** Proxmox VE clusters, LXC containers, VMs, and automated backup workflows.
* **Networking & Security:** VLAN segmentation, reverse proxies, firewall hardening, and mesh VPNs.
* **Self-Hosting & Containers:** Docker Compose stacks, media servers, and local-first tools.
* **Automation & Linux:** Infrastructure-as-code with Ansible, Fedora Linux workflows, and Windows/Active Directory lab administration.
* **Side Projects:** Occasional detours into local LLMs, custom Android utilities, and Wear OS watch faces.

## A Quick Note on Documentation

All guides here are sanitized for public viewing—internal IP addresses, hostnames, and private domains have been replaced with generic placeholders. Everything documented is tested in my own lab, though your mileage may vary depending on your hardware and versions.
